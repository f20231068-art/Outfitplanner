# Niche seller allow-list (100 menswear brands, India)

Researched 2026-10-04 for the stylist agent, to replace Myntra, Amazon, AJIO, Flipkart, Tata CLiQ and Nykaa.
These are brand-owned or specialist stores: Indian D2C startups, heritage labels, ethnic and handloom
specialists, a few curated multi-brand boutiques, and the official stores of global labels sold in India.

## How each entry was checked

Every domain was fetched on 2026-10-04. It had to answer with a real storefront: HTTP 200, clothing words and
prices in the page, and no "coming soon", parked-domain or injected-spam markers. Candidates that failed these
checks were dropped (placeholder pages, parked or for-sale domains, a spam-injected site, wrong domains). "blocked bot" means the site refuses
scripted requests (HTTP 403), so it was kept on the strength of the brand being well known, not on a page check.
Re-run a check before relying on any entry long term: small D2C sites change domains and shut down.
